How a photo becomes a result

Three independent specialist detectors, not one shared model, because the training data for products, gaps, and tags never overlaps. Merging them into one model would teach it that products are "background" wherever gap or tag data was labeled. Each runs separately, then fuses into one result.

Why three detectors, not one

A unified model trained on the union of all three datasets would see zero product boxes in every gap-labeled image, and vice versa. It would learn "no label here" means "nothing to detect," actively suppressing recall. Training three single-class specialists on only the data where their class is exhaustively labeled avoids that trap entirely.
